## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a gentle, restorative, and frictionless financial tracking experience. Transforming the daily chore of bookkeeping into an intimate, warm ritual, it draws heavily upon modern soft-minimalism and pastel macaron aesthetics. 

The emotional tone balances delight and clarity: reassuring rather than intimidating, airy rather than cluttered, and approachable rather than formal. Interfaces prioritize rapid data capture (3-tap logging) encased within generous negative space, pillow-soft container geometry, and warm ambient backdrops that alleviate financial anxiety.

## Colors

The palette revolves around confectionery macaron pastels anchored by warm, high-legibility plum neutrals to prevent visual wash-out.

### Primary & Brand Accents
- **Primary Pink (`#FF7BA9`)**: Central interactive brand anchor; drives primary CTAs, active tab accents, and high-priority indicators.
- **Deep Pink (`#F75C8A`)**: Pressed/hover states and critical highlighted moments.
- **Soft Pink (`#FFD6E5`)**: Secondary badges, subtle pill selections, and input hover rings.
- **Faint Blush (`#FFECF1`)**: Inner container fills, chip base layers, and subtle selected states.

### Macaron Categorical Palette
- **Peach (`#FFB3C1`)**: Dining, cafe, and personal grooming.
- **Cream Butter (`#FFD98E`)**: Shopping, rewards, and leisure.
- **Mint Sage (`#8FE0C3`)**: Health, fitness, and groceries.
- **Taro Violet (`#C9B4E8`)**: Subscriptions, digital assets, and recurring bills.
- **Sky Dew (`#9FD3E8`)**: Travel, commute, and utilities.
- **Soft Coral (`#FFA891`)**: Gifts, seasonal expenses, and home.

### Semantic & Status
- **Expense (`#FF6B8A`)**: Clear, non-punitive crimson pink for expense flows.
- **Income (`#5EC99A`)**: Soft herbal green for income and savings growth.
- **Budget Warning (`#FFB347`)**: Warm apricot for nearing budget thresholds.
- **Overbudget (`#FF5C7A`)**: Vibrant rose red for urgent overrun indicators.

### Neutrals & Canvas
- **Primary Text (`#4A3F44`)**: Warm charcoal-plum providing accessible contrast without cold, clinical harshness.
- **Secondary Text (`#9B8A92`)**: Muted mauve for captions, timestamps, and placeholder labels.
- **Border / Divider (`#F2E6EC`)**: Whisper-light milky border for boundary structure.
- **Canvas Base (`#FFF7F9`)**: Creamy milk-pink background creating a cozy visual cocoon.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure cloud-white surfaces to pop cleanly from the tinted canvas.

## Typography

The type system is powered entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its gentle geometric structure, curved terminals, and open aperture counters that align with the friendly personality.

- **Financial Figures**: Prominent numbers utilize `display-num` with semi-tabular formatting (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure strict visual alignment across ledger lists and balance dashboards.
- **Hierarchy Structure**: Display titles keep character tracking tight (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to create compact, solid blocks of text, while captions maintain positive micro-tracking (`+0.01em` to `+0.02em`) for clarity on small mobile displays.
- **Weight Pairing**: Headlines favor 600–700 weights to establish confident structural stops, balanced by 400–500 weight body text in warm `#4A3F44` to ensure sustained reading comfort.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts are designed for single-handed mobile usage with an adaptive container structure for larger canvases.

- **Grid Model**: A fluid 4-column layout on mobile devices (`<600px`) transitioning to 8 columns on tablets and 12 columns on desktop web/dashboards.
- **Margins & Safe Zones**: Canvas side margins default to `1rem` (16px) on mobile for edge-to-edge touch target maximization, expanding to `1.5rem` (24px) on tablet and dynamic auto-centering on desktop screens (max content width `480px` for mobile web view or `1024px` for expansive budget analytics).
- **Rhythm**: Internal card gaps follow a strict 8px/16px baseline (`space-sm` for related micro items, `space-md` for row separators, and `space-xl` between modular sections).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses soft ambient diffusion instead of harsh directional drop shadows, mirroring the physical presence of velvet or frosted pastel ceramics.

- **Canvas Level (0dp)**: Surface background `#FFF7F9` tinted with gentle milk-pink warmth.
- **Card Tier 1 (Low Elevation)**: White `#FFFFFF` surfaces paired with a tinted ambient bloom: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(247, 92, 138, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(74, 63, 68, 0.03)`. Outlines use 1px solid `#F2E6EC`.
- **Floating Controls (Interactive Elevation)**: Floating action buttons (e.g., Quick Add "+") and active bottom navigation bars use a deeper diffused spread: `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -4px rgba(255, 123, 169, 0.35)`.
- **Modals & Overlays**: Backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) over 20% alpha `#4A3F44` with bottom-sheet entry animations mimicking tactile cushions.

## Shapes

The shape architecture relies on oversized pill contours and generous corner sweeps (Level 3 Pill-Shaped system), creating a soft, tactile, touch-friendly UI.

- **Dashboard & List Cards**: Encased with `rounded-lg` (24px to 32px radii), giving every content group a cushion-like profile.
- **Buttons & Interactive Tags**: Standard primary buttons and quick-entry category tags use full pill roundness (`border-radius: 9999px` or standard token radius 16px–24px).
- **Icon Enclosures**: Category glyph containers are rounded squares (`rounded-lg` at 18px–20px) or full circles to maintain geometric harmony.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add / Submit)**: Solid fill `#FF7BA9` with white `#FFFFFF` text, 48px height, full pill shape (`rounded-full`), subtly elevated with pink ambient shadow. Active state depresses gently (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Background `#FFECF1`, text color `#F75C8A`, zero hard borders, providing a pillowy secondary touchpoint.

### Category Chips & Quick Selectors
- **Unselected**: White `#FFFFFF` or `#FFECF1` background, `#9B8A92` text, 1px `#F2E6EC` border.
- **Selected**: Pastel category tone background (e.g., `#FFD6E5` for personal, `#8FE0C3` at 20% alpha for groceries) with matching primary tinted border and accent badge.

### Transaction Ledger Lists
- **Rows**: Contained in white surface cards. Each entry features an icon pill (40px) colored in its category hue, followed by 2-line vertical metadata (Category + Note in `body-md`, Timestamp in `label-sm`).
- **Amounts**: Tabular numeric format right-aligned. Expenses prefixed with `-` in `#FF6B8A`, income with `+` in `#5EC99A`. Dividers use 1px `#F2E6EC` offset by 56px to skip icon boundaries.

### Input Fields & Keypads
- **Amount Field**: Display-sized typography (`display-num`), center-aligned or bold left-aligned, zero border lines, sitting cleanly over a faint background `#FFECF1` container with 20px padding.
- **Quick Numpad**: Generous touch targets (56px minimum height) with rounded corners (16px), gentle tactile press feedback, and integrated macaron color badges for immediate category tagging.

### Cards & Budget Progress Bars
- **Monthly Summary Card**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with 24px border radius. Contains large current balance and two internal pill pods for Month Expense (`#FFECF1`) and Month Income (`rgba(94, 201, 154, 0.12)`).
- **Progress Track**: Rounded track in `#F2E6EC` filled with gradient-free solid tones transitioning from `#5EC99A` (under 75%) to `#FFB347` (75–99%) and `#FF5C7A` (100%+).
